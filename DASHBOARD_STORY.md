# Dashboard Stakeholder Stories

1. City Operations Lead

Headline

There is a high rate of SLA breaches in Bengaluru among the six cities.

Evidence

Bengaluru has the maximum number of bookings with 107, and 17 SLA breaches, which makes up to 15.89%. The second is Hyderabad with 14.29%, then Mumbai with 14.14%, Chennai with 13.27%, Pune with 13%, and Delhi NCR with the lowest rate at 7.23%.

Implication

City Ops Lead should focus more attention on the operational reasons for the high percentage of SLA breaches in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Mumbai. It might be helpful to compare the booked services in these cities to see if there are any particular issues.

2. Category Lead

Headline

The most profitable category is Deep Home Cleaning, which also has the highest booking volumes.

Evidence

The most booked service is a Deep Home Cleaning with 176, which brings the revenue to ₹508,964. The second is a Salon for Women with 65 bookings and ₹151,689, followed by an Electrical Repair with 112 bookings and ₹119,881. An AC Repair & Service has 74,80,837. A Plumbing service has 95 bookings and ₹80,837, and a Salon for Men has 78 bookings and ₹73,563.

Implication

For the Category Lead, Deep Home Cleaning booking dominates the volumes and the revenues. There is no doubt that the attention to other categories, for instance, Salon for Women and Salon for Men, should be increased.
